Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Tasksfy

What is Tasksfy?

Tasksfy is a HR outsourcing app that connects clients with trusted service providers offering blue-collar services including plumbers, electricians, cleaners, and more.

Where is Tasksfy currently available?

Based on our research, we're initially focusing on Nanyuki where 75% of medium-based businesses and 60% of households outsource blue-collar services regularly.

Do we match the Market Standard?

Yes, we do. We are a team of experienced professionals who are dedicated to providing the best service to our clients.

How do I find a reliable service provider?

Tasksfy provides a platform with vetted service providers, client reviews, ratings, and potential background checks to ensure reliability.

How long does it take to find help through Tasksfy?

Unlike traditional methods that take an average of 45 minutes, Tasksfy matches you with qualified providers quickly using filters and location services.

How are service prices determined?

Tasksfy offers standardized pricing structures for services, allowing you to choose from pre-defined options for transparency.

How do I register as a service provider?

After registration, you can select up to 3 skills. Approval takes 3-5 business days before you can start receiving task requests.

How does Tasksfy help me get more clients?

Tasksfy connects you with a consistent stream of potential clients, addressing the challenge 73% of providers face in finding new clients.

What commission does Tasksfy take?

Tasksfy charges 5% of the task amount deposited in escrow after the client approves your proposal.

How does Tasksfy make money?

We charge a 5% commission on each completed transaction between clients and service providers.

What's unique about Tasksfy's approach?

Unlike competitors, Tasksfy is a one-stop app for various blue-collar services and crowdsources providers while vetting them, allowing people to be their own bosses.
